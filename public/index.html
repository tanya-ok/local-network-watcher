Packet inspectionCapture engine is a later milestone

This monitor lists socket endpoints and process-level byte/packet counters that macOS makes visible to your account. It does not capture packet contents, decrypt encrypted traffic or identify a connection as harmful. Packet capture needs an explicitly started local capture engine. Blocking and isolation need a separate macOS Network Extension.
