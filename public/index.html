Process control

After inspection and confirmation: stop (SIGTERM), stop a previewed process tree, pause (SIGSTOP) and resume (SIGCONT) for processes owned by your account. Restart is offered only for launchd jobs in your user domain. Every action is written to a local action log.

Packet inspectionManual TShark capture, headers only, memory only

This monitor lists socket endpoints and process-level byte/packet counters that macOS makes visible to your account. It does not capture packet contents, decrypt encrypted traffic or identify a connection as harmful. Packet capture runs only when you start it on the Capture tab, for 5 to 30 seconds. Blocking and isolation need a separate macOS Network Extension.
